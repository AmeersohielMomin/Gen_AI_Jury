Can a Conditional GAN Improve Minority-Class Skin Lesion Detection?

We compare three leakage-safe EfficientNet-B0 arms on 3,664 held-out real lesions to isolate synthetic image diversity from class-frequency repetition — and audit why a 128px DCGAN failed to beat the real-only baseline.

Jury Key Finding

Real-Only Baseline Outperformed cGAN Augmentation

real_only achieved 0.354 SCC F1 (PR-AUC 0.340) vs. 0.308 for cgan_scc. Checkerboard artifacts and discriminator saturation produced a 0.928 real-vs-fake detector AUC, illustrating synthetic domain shift.
